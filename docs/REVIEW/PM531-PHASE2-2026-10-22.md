## 5. Critical path and recommended reset

The final path is accepted CLI/platform behavior + accepted INGEST, then accepted
safe make/elevation/app integration, then family-platform installation/error checks
and witnessed G2. Host authoring can overlap, as the plan permits; native safety
and final acceptance cannot be inferred from an image demo.

**First priority: resolve the feasibility/authority decisions that block that path.**
For Windows and Mac, identify a concrete ordinary-settings method for device-access
visibility and one authorized session bound before writes. State exactly what
works, what has failed and what cannot currently be established. If issued
requirements cannot be met on available platforms, PM must present an explicit
design/method/contract decision, with its disk-safety consequences and reserved
approvals, rather than silently weakening safety or commissioning endless probes.

**Second: restore delivery focus.** Track the actual INGEST459 closure separately
from S1 diagnostic proof; retain the independent whole-HOST gaps. Make S3 feasibility,
then real child/elevation/common-path integration the next visible product outcomes
when their prerequisites are cleared. An image-backed rehearsal may show UI/plumbing
when independently scoped, but would not satisfy real-card/native/G2 acceptance.

**Third: constrain proof and crash work by the decision it closes.** Before another
numerical-proof queue, map each residual to a specific criterion and choose a finite
defensible method or explicit design decision. Before another crash run, settle
whether the evidence is needed to accept current code, choose Q-P2-1 architecture,
or qualify later batching. Scope a valid bounded model/campaign accordingly, while
preserving every known gap. A generic process supervisor should not become the
unexamined prerequisite for the entire desktop product.

Measure future rounds by a usable capability integrated, a named acceptance
criterion closed, or a concrete feasibility/architecture decision resolved.
Require runner preflight and outcome/cost rationale before campaigns. Repeated
correction classes trigger a mechanism/design review, not another cosmetic issue.
These are recommendations for discussion, not new role assignments.
