## CS-2 — edge-test consumable and apparatus

Section 1500.49(e) specifies pressure-sensitive TFE high-temperature electrical
insulation tape described by MIL-I-23594B (1971):

- backing thickness 0.0026–0.0035 inch;
- pressure-sensitive silicone-polymer adhesive, nominal thickness 0.003 inch;
- width not less than 1/4 inch (6 mm);
- tape temperature 70–80 °F during testing;
- one unstretched layer around the full mandrel circumference, ends butted or
  overlapped no more than 0.10 inch.

The steel mandrel is 0.375 ±0.005 inch in diameter, at least Rockwell C 40, with
surface roughness no greater than 16 microinches and no scratches, nicks or burrs.
Tangential velocity is 1.00 ±0.08 inch/s through the center 75 percent of one
revolution, with smooth start/stop. Apply up to 1.35 lbf normal to the mandrel axis,
hold the axis 90° ±5° to the edge/tangent, prevent linear motion and seek the
worst-case orientation. A complete tape cut at least 1/2 inch long identifies a
sharp edge. The official drawing visually confirms the one-wrap, force, angle,
single-revolution and worst-case-orientation relationships.

## CS-4 — scope and exemptions

Both methods screen accessible hazards before and after the referenced use-and-abuse
tests, excluding each section's bite test. They exempt bicycles and full-size and
non-full-size cribs governed by the named parts. They also exempt necessarily
functional sharp features when no nonfunctional sharp feature exists; toys require
a conspicuous, legible, visible sales label. Section 1500.48 applies this to points;
§ 1500.49 applies it to metal or glass edges and defines those material classes.
Digital-Tape has no planned functional sharp point or edge, so these exemptions do
not create a project pass condition.

Probe B access rules include:

- insert to the collar when the opening's minor dimension is smaller than the collar;
- for an opening larger than the collar but smaller than 9.00 inches, insert with the
  extension in any direction up to 2.25 times the opening's minor dimension;
- at 9.00 inches or larger, depth is unrestricted subject to encountered sub-openings;
- an adjacent gap no greater than 0.020 inch is inaccessible without probe testing.

Section 1500.48 uses a 0.040 by 0.045-inch gaging slot, sensing head recessed at least
0.015 inch, a further 0.005-inch travel against a 0.5 lbf spring, insertion from every
accessible direction at no more than 1.00 lbf, and a lit indicator as the sharp result.

## CS-5 — applicable use and abuse

Section 1500.50 requires the applicable age-band procedure, the most stringent band
when ages span bands or labeling is unclear, English units, previously untested
samples except tension follows torque on the same sample, preconditioning for at
least four hours at 73 ±3 °F and 20–70 percent relative humidity, test start within
five minutes after removal, stated assembled/disassembled treatment, and the most
severe reasonable direction of application.

For the intended seven-year-old band, § 1500.53 includes:

- for toys under 10.0 ±0.01 lb, four random-orientation drops from 3 ft ±0.5 inch
  onto nominal 1/8-inch type-IV vinyl-composition tile over at least 2.5 inches of
  concrete, impact area at least 3 square feet, with examination after every drop;
- 4.0 ±0.2 in-lb torque, clockwise and counterclockwise, applied within five seconds
  to 180 degrees or the torque limit and held ten seconds;
- 15.0 ±0.5 lbf tension parallel and then perpendicular to the major axis, each
  applied within five seconds and held ten seconds, on the same sample used for torque;
- 30.0 ±0.5 lbf compression through the specified 1.125 ±0.015-inch rigid metal disc,
  applied within five seconds and held ten seconds;
- the section's conditional bite and flexure methods where their application clauses
  are met.

Hardware's deterministic twelve-orientation 1.00 m bare-concrete screen is useful
internal engineering work but is not equivalent to the four random drops on the
specified tile/concrete medium. It also does not cover torque, tension, compression,
conditional bite or flexure. Those remain explicit regulatory-method gaps; severity
in one dimension does not establish equivalence.
