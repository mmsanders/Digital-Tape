Why replay.py cannot close over a FAILING product run.

replay.py recomputes errors = hardened.check(...) only. runner.py's own
error list additionally carries runner-level strings such as the adapter's
nonzero exit, and the comparison is exact. So the two can never agree on a
run whose adapter exited nonzero. Every hash binding, the spec
authentication and the independent regeneration of the input fixture all
PASS; only the verdict-string comparison differs, by exactly one string.
