# WP-25 — ruggedization and abuse testing

**Stream:** hardware · **Owner:** Hardware drafts/tests; Michael performs physical
trials; Verification reviews criteria and audits evidence · **Status:** in flight
**Depends on:** WP-04, WP-22, WP-23, WP-24 · **Blocks:** finished mechanical design,
WP-31 and child field use

## Interface

Ruggedization is a system design input for the assembled player and loaded cartridge,
not a cosmetic enclosure pass. Hardware must define the shock/load path through the
shell, transport, cartridge interface, boards, battery, connectors and controls before
final enclosure CAD. Energy absorption must not trade away transport alignment,
serviceability or cartridge retention.

The performance requirement is mechanism-neutral. PETG shells, TPU bumpers, foam,
ribs, compliant subframes, locking connectors, strain relief, service loops and
captured fasteners are candidates, not mandated solutions. Precision transport parts
may require rigid location even when the enclosure uses compliant protection.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Architecture before detailed CAD.** Publish a failure-mode/mitigation matrix for
   the loaded player covering at least drops, shaking/rattle, repeated handling,
   battery retention, board/connector retention, cartridge retention, controls,
   transport alignment, openings and fasteners. Each mitigation names an inspection
   or functional check that can detect failure.
2. **No hazardous outcome.** After any qualifying abuse trial there is no exposed or
   loose battery, exposed live conductor, sharp fracture, hazardous detached part,
   unintended enclosure opening or cartridge ejection. A cosmetic blemish may pass;
   a hidden loose part, intermittent power/audio/control fault or changed transport
   alignment does not.
3. **Drop family.** The pre-test plan covers the loaded unit on every face and the
   worst-case edges/corners from a declared height no lower than 1.0 m onto a declared
   hard surface. Hardware must propose the exact orientation count, sequence, height,
   surface and pass checks for PM approval before trials. WP-24's cartridge-shell S-3
   remains a separate minimum and is not consumed by a player-shell pass.
4. **Shake/rough-play family.** The pre-test plan defines a reproducible duration,
   orientation/profile and fixture or manual protocol that can reveal loose hardware,
   connector walkout, battery motion, card/cartridge release and control/transport
   faults. “Shook it and it seemed fine” is not evidence.
5. **Staged risk.** First execute dummy-mass or inert-component enclosure trials.
   Repeat the approved sequence on a representative assembled unit in the intended
   material/process before mechanical acceptance. Do not use a live lithium cell in
   an exploratory destructive trial; charging and fabrication holds remain separate.
6. **Evidence.** Preserve pre/post mass and inspection, photographs, material/vendor,
   printer, orientation, slicer/settings, fastener state, test order, raw observations,
   functional checks, every failure and every repair. A repaired design restarts the
   affected sequence; it does not erase earlier failures.
7. **Negative controls.** Before the final run, demonstrate that at least one retained
   check detects a deliberately loosened/corrupted representative condition such as a
   backed-out connector, released captive mass or altered retention feature. The
   control must not damage a live cell or create an unsafe test setup.

## Hardware's proposal against these criteria (18 Sep 2026)

`spec/hw/ruggedization.md` **rev 0.1** is Hardware's response. It is a proposal for PM approval
and Verification review, **not evidence and not a result** — no enclosure exists, no mass has
been weighed and no trial is authorized.

| Criterion | Where it is answered | State |
|---|---|---|
| A-1 architecture before CAD | §1 load path, §2 failure matrix — 18 modes, each with a detection check | **Proposed** |
| A-2 no hazardous outcome | §4 rules R-1 to R-8, with the cosmetic line drawn explicitly | **Proposed** |
| A-3 drop family | §3.2 — 1.0 m, declared concrete surface, 12 orientations, cumulative on one unit, n=2 | **Proposed; needs PM approval before any trial** |
| A-4 shake/rough-play | §3.3 — metronome-paced shake, 120 s per axis on three axes, plus 25 tumbles | **Proposed** |
| A-5 staged risk | §3.1 — dummy mass first, representative unit second, **inert cell in both** | **Proposed** |
| A-6 evidence | §5 — pre-run record, functional card, post-run record, failures kept | **Proposed** |
| A-7 negative controls | §3.4 — C-1 omitted cell-retainer fastener, C-2 backed-out shell fastener, each with the check that must catch it and what it means if it does not | **Proposed** |

Two things the proposal deliberately does not do. **It does not schedule a live-cell trial**, or
leave room for one to be read into it. And it **does not treat the loaded mass as known**: ~150 g
is an estimate carried from the thermal budget, and if the measured mass is more than 20% off,
the height and surface go back to PM rather than being adjusted quietly.

**The staging is gated on the printer.** No abuse trial may use a printed unit until the
calibration coupons in `hardware/printing/owned-printer-baseline.md` §4 have been run and
reviewed — an abuse result from an uncharacterized process cannot be told apart from a bad print.
