## Real sensor boundary

Linux/Python3 is the declared measured route. `census` lstat-censuses the complete
owned root, including dot files, directories and retained failed trees. It charges
st_blocks*512 PER PATH, so aliases overcount; paths/device/inode/link count/logical/
allocated bytes are returned. Links are not followed; escaping links or unsupported/
unmeasurable nodes fail closed. Directory allocation is included. Scan/receipt
growth is reserved separately; archive compression is not assumed as headroom.
Separate real sparse/hardlink/temp/archive probes retain all original files. The
missing-root sensor failure is terminal for that probe session, while the complete
task namespace remains measurable and is accounted after archive creation.

Integer CPU nanoseconds conservatively ceil OS usage representations. Completed
RUSAGE_SELF plus RUSAGE_CHILDREN charges reaped children once. Live known child
group utime/stime ticks are sampled separately and never added after wait4 to the
completed counter. OS wait4 reports the actual direct helper and waited-for child
usage; this helper contract forbids descendants. Unexpected descendants stop the
owned group; no claim of complete accounting for arbitrary orphan/double-fork or
foreign processes. The method's arbitrary-descendant accounting requirement is
therefore a precise LIMITATION of this implementation, not silently satisfied.
Future C adapters must independently demonstrate a bounded single-child process
route or supply separately reviewed descendant accounting before use. Generic
native argv are not cleared by these harmless probes.

The supervisor polls at10ms, records real CPU/storage/wall, kills the exact owned
process group on overrun/timeout/measurement failure and wait4-reaps its known
direct child. Poll scheduling, scanner work, native kernel I/O and kill/reap latency
have NO hard finite CPU/storage overshoot bound. No zero-overshoot, power-loss,
host-native/security or arbitrary-program theorem is claimed. Completion reserve
does not itself prove that a running child cannot exceed it. All observations and
incomplete stops survive; a final measurement failure is not permission to retry.

`Session.storage` retains task/phase high-water increments and refuses replacing
the immutable baseline. The real probes exercise it; a production phase transition
adapter is NOT implemented here. Fixture gates do not authenticate real Product
