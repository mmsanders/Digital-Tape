# Sizing study — not a design

Open `layout-study.step` in Bambu Studio (it imports STEP). Grey outlines are
envelopes; coloured blocks are parts. Left to right:

1. **A — two decks stacked** in thickness (blue)
2. **B — two decks side by side** in height (orange)
3. Ghost envelopes of the **TPS-L2**, **WM-2** and **WM-F5**

Source: `hardware/cad/sizing/layout_study.py`. Part sizes are tagged there:
`[REPO]` from this repository, `[EST]` my estimate — **only the cartridge size is
from the repo, and it is itself a working number (SH-1)**. The Walkman figures are
search-index summaries attributed to walkman.land, which this environment cannot
open.

| B — side by side | 92 × 154 × 24.5 | 347 cm³ |
