# A1MINI-BTN-02 — does the button latch at all?

**Print `plate.stl` on your A1 Mini. 0.4 mm nozzle, Bambu PLA Basic, stock
profile, no supports, nothing swapped mid-print.**
Hardware Lead · rev 2, 23 Sep 2026 · replaces the WP04 button test, which could
not have worked

---

## Why there is a rev 2

You were right that new buttons were needed, and the box too. The old bar channel
was cut through the middle of where the button sits — **the bar and the button
wanted the same piece of space.** That is why it did not go together, and why
moving the box did not help: the little hook sticks out past the front of the
button, so the bar had to move to the *front*, not the box to the side.

The bar has moved. There is now a check in the build that refuses to produce a
plate if the two ever overlap again.

## What you get

Four buttons — **A, B, C, N** — one frame, one bar. The letters are 7 mm now.

## What to do

1. Drop the **bar** into the two slots in the posts, from the front.
2. Drop a **button** into the square hole. Its wide top rests on the posts.
3. **Press it down** with your thumb until something happens.
4. **Pull the bar forward** (toward you) and see if the button comes free.

For each of the four, tell me:

- **did it click and stay down?** — yes / no / sort of
- **how hard was the press?** — light / firm / too hard
- **did pulling the bar release it?** — yes / no / had to force it
- anything that broke

Hold the bar with a finger while you press — there is no spring in this, and
nothing to buy. The button staying down *is* the latch working. It coming free
when you pull the bar *is* the release working.

## N is the one that matters most

**N has no hook at all. It must NOT stay down.** If N clicks and holds, the frame
is gripping it by friction and every other answer on this plate is worthless.
Please try N properly rather than skipping it.

## What this cannot tell us

Whether any of these depths is the *right* depth. Nothing has ever latched, so
this round only asks whether the mechanism works. Ranking comes after something
works. It is also not a strength, life or safety test, and four buttons pressed by
hand is not a measurement of anything.

If all four fail, that is a clean answer too — send a photo and stop, and we
change the mechanism rather than the numbers.
